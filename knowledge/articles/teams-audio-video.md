## Resolution
1. **Teams > Settings > Devices**: select the right headset, microphone and camera, then make a test call.
2. **Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone / Camera**: allow access for desktop apps.
3. Clear the cache: quit Teams, then reset the app in **Settings > Apps > Microsoft Teams > Advanced options > Reset**.
4. Update the audio driver from **Software Center**.

If the headset works in other apps but not in Teams, step 2 is the usual cause.
